## Answer

**Yes.** Caddy has raised its minimum Go version specifically because of quic-go (the library that provides its HTTP/3 support), and it did so at least three times. Each time, the Go minimum bump and the quic-go upgrade landed in the *same* commit, with the commit message explicitly tying them together. The reason is visible in the diffs: quic-go depends on a per-Go-version fork of `crypto/tls` (`quic-go/qtls-go1-NN` / formerly `marten-seemann/qtls-go1-NN`), so upgrading quic-go forces Caddy to require the matching new Go release.

### Primary citing commit: `f45a6de20dd19e82e58c85b37e03957b2203b544`

**"go.mod: Update quic-go to v0.37.0, bump to Go 1.20 minimum (#5644)"** (Jul 21, 2023, authored by Marten Seemann — the quic-go maintainer, itself a strong signal this was driven by the HTTP/3 library).

The commit message explicitly couples the two changes:

```
go.mod: Update quic-go to v0.37.0, bump to Go 1.20 minimum (#5644)

* update quic-go to v0.37.0
* Bump to Go 1.20
```

And the diff performs the bump of the `go` directive right alongside the quic-go upgrade and the removal of the old Go 1.19 TLS fork:

+go 1.20
