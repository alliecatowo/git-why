## Answer

**Yes.** ripgrep's special ignore file was not always called `.ignore`. It went through two earlier names:

1. **`.xrepignore`** — the original name, back when the tool was called "xrep" (the earliest implementation, commit `065c449`, documented `$HOME/.xrepignore`).
2. **`.rgignore`** — introduced when "xrep" was renamed to "ripgrep".
3. **`.ignore`** — the current name, adopted in September 2016. (There was also `.agignore` in the list briefly, for Silver Searcher compatibility, later removed.)
