**Yes.** ripgrep's special ignore-file name used to be **`.rgignore`**, not `.ignore`.

### Additional corroboration from history

The name `.rgignore` goes back to ripgrep's very first commits — `git grep` at `a744ec1` ("Rename xrep to ripgrep.") shows `.rgignore` in `src/ignore.rs` (`const IGNORE_NAMES` containing `".rgignore"`, plus `$HOME/.rgignore` in a doc comment). Commit `cc90511` (dated 2016-09-23) then switched the canonical name to `.ignore`, while deliberately keeping `.rgignore` support for backward compatibility until the next semver bump.
