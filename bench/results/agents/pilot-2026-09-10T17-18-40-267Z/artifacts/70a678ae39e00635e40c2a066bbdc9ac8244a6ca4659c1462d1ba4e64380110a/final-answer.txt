**Yes.** ripgrep's special ignore-file name was not always `.ignore`. It used to be **`.rgignore`** — and even earlier, when the tool was called *xrep*, it was **`.xrepignore`** (with `.agignore` also briefly in the list alongside it).

### The commit that establishes the answer

**`cc90511ab29d3c37e873acac0fd80f90473f54bb`** — *"Switch from .rgignore to .ignore."* (Andrew Gallant, 2016-09-23)

This is the commit that changed the canonical name from `.rgignore` to `.ignore`. Its full commit message states the switch directly:

> ```
> Switch from .rgignore to .ignore.
>
> But don't actually remove support for .rgignore until the next semver
> bump.
>
> Note that this puts us in line with the silver searcher:
> https://github.com/ggreer/the_silver_searcher/pull/974
>
> Fixes #40
> ```

And the diff itself shows the old name being replaced by the new one in the ignore-file list (`src/ignore.rs`):
