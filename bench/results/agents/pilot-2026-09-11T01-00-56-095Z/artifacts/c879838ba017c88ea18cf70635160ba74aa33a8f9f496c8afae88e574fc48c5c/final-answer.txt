Yes, ripgrep's special ignore-file name used to be `.rgignore` in its early days.

It was changed to `.ignore` (while maintaining support for `.rgignore` for backward compatibility) in commit `cc90511`.

**Supporting quote from commit `cc90511`:**

> Switch from .rgignore to .ignore.
> 
> But don't actually remove support for .rgignore until the next semver
> bump.
> 
> Note that this puts us in line with the silver searcher:
> https://github.com/ggreer/the_silver_searcher/pull/974
