The requests library switched its default text-encoding guesser on Python 3 from `chardet` to `charset_normalizer` to **remove license ambiguity** for downstream projects. 

Specifically, `chardet` is licensed under the **LGPL** (Lesser General Public License). While using it as a separate dependency is fine for `requests` itself, it created licensing/legal ambiguity for downstream projects—especially those that bundle/repackage `requests` and its dependencies into a single binary or artifact (such as `docker-compose`). In those cases, it was unclear whether the resulting bundled artifact would also have to be licensed under the LGPL. Replacing `chardet` with `charset_normalizer`, which is **MIT-licensed**, resolved this issue.
