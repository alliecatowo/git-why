The requests library switched its default text-encoding guesser on Python 3 from `chardet` to `charset_normalizer` primarily to **resolve licensing concerns for downstream projects**. 

`chardet` is licensed under the LGPL (Lesser General Public License), which creates licensing ambiguity and potential compliance issues for downstream projects that bundle or compile `requests` alongside its dependencies into a single artifact/binary (such as `docker-compose`). In contrast, `charset_normalizer` is licensed under the permissive **MIT License**. Swapping `chardet` for `charset_normalizer` as the default on Python 3 removed this license ambiguity for downstream consumers.
