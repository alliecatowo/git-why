Yes, ripgrep's special ignore-file used to be called **`.rgignore`** in the early days. Additionally, support for **`.agignore`** was also briefly included before being removed.

The transition to **`.ignore`** is established by the following commits:

### 1. Transition from `.rgignore` to `.ignore`
* **Commit SHA:** `cc90511ab29d3c37e873acac0fd80f90473f54bb`
* **Commit Message:**
  ```text
  Switch from .rgignore to .ignore.
  
  But don't actually remove support for .rgignore until the next semver
  bump.
  
  Note that this puts us in line with the silver searcher:
  https://github.com/ggreer/the_silver_searcher/pull/974
  
  Fixes #40
  ```
* **Supporting Diff (from `src/ignore.rs`):**
  ```rust
  const IGNORE_NAMES: &'static [&'static str] = &[
      ".gitignore",
  +   ".ignore",
      ".rgignore",
  ];
  ```

---

### 2. Removal of `.agignore`
* **Commit SHA:** `7cefc55238bc23f8b329410520242046dea9ef33`
* **Commit Message:**
  ```text
  Remove .agignore from ignore file list.
  ```
* **Supporting Diff (from `src/ignore.rs`):**
  ```rust
  const IGNORE_NAMES: &'static [&'static str] = &[
      ".gitignore",
  -   ".agignore",
      ".rgignore",
  ];
  ```
