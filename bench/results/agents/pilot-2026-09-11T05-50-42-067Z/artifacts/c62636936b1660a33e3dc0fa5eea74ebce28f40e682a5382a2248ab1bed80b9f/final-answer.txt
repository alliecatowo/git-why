The `requests` library switched its default text-encoding guesser from `chardet` to `charset_normalizer` (for Python 3) to **remove license ambiguity** for downstream projects. Specifically, `chardet` is licensed under the LGPL (Lesser General Public License), which created legal uncertainty for projects that bundled/packaged `requests` and its dependencies (like `chardet`) into a single binary/artifact, as they might be forced to comply with the LGPL. Switching to the MIT-licensed `charset_normalizer` resolved this issue.
